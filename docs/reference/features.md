# How each feature works

The detail behind briefs, cards, /ask, /wow, /text, and /new: what was built, how it was verified, and the calls Blake made along the way. Read the section for the feature being changed. Dollar figures for questions and small jobs here were measured on the earlier small model (before 2026-11-11); the `cost` command shows current spend. Since 2026-11-11, approving a text needs `TEXT_APPROVERS`, not `AI_USERS`; `/remind` is described in deal-spaces-and-routing.md.

## Roamer briefs
- Briefs hold a Roamer's personal details, so they post only where the reader should see them: a direct message with Basecamp, or the deal's own space, for that deal only ("brief <deal#>" typed there, built 2026-11-12); asking there for another deal points to a direct message or that deal's own space. In the space the Roamer already picked for the brief is used, and the contact picker is never posted there.
- Both brief calls stop after 3 minutes and retry once; the lists the instructions cap (8 Wow, 8 commitments, 5 excites, 5 open questions, 5 tags, 4 key facts) are also trimmed in code before the brief is saved (2026-11-12).
- When a deal has more than one contact, Basecamp asks which is the Roamer rather than guessing. The picked contact must still be on the deal when the brief runs.
- Every deal should have its Roamer attached. A deal with none is a HubSpot problem: the requester is told it needs fixing in HubSpot, and a WARNING "deal has no Roamer contact" is logged for an alert. Basecamp does not reroute around it.
- A typed "brief" always starts a brief. Only Home taps have the two-minute repeat check (a typed repeat check was tried and removed: it promised briefs in spaces that never got them).
- The Roamer brief (full reads and updates) runs at low effort (Blake's call, 2026-11-08). Low and medium were compared on three Utah deals (90, 153, 228 items): low cost 20 to 30% less and arrived 2 to 3 times sooner (16 to 19 seconds), with the same Journey items and up to 2 fewer Mods and 1 fewer Wow; Blake read them side by side and found low acceptable. At low effort most of a brief's cost is reading the record, not writing. Tried and not worth it: grouping the record by day (5 to 18% less reading, but the model wrote more, so it cost the same or more). Shortening words in the record saves nothing and garbles quotes. Cost levers still open: drop automated messages in code before the model sees them (2 to 10% of the record, rules need care).
- The brief's last list is Commitments and next steps, not Journey (Blake's call, 2026-11-08): what a Team Member promised and next steps due from either side, soonest first. Gear the Roamer brings goes in Who they are, worries in Open questions, timing in Journey so far. Notes written with the old JOURNEY list are not reused, so each deal's next brief is a full read.
- **Incremental brief updates: live and verified 2026-11-07.** On 51455: full read 32 items, 9,938 tokens in, 3,564 out, 35 seconds; then an update with 1 new item, 4,991 in, 1,804 out, 17 seconds (about half on each; the input floor is the instructions plus the saved brief). `planBrief` in `src/briefJob.js` decides: reuse (nothing changed), update (only items newer than the saved brief's newest item; `updateBrief` in `src/agents/roamerBrief.js` sends the saved brief plus those items and gets back the whole brief, `new_since`, and a `rebuild` flag), or full. Full when: no saved brief or no history in the database, a different Roamer, 10 updates since the last full read, 30 days since it (or no recorded full read yet, so every deal's first brief after this deploy is full), an older item removed or added late, an older item edited after the brief was written (`hs_lastmodifieddate`; closes the call summary gap), or the update fails or asks for a full read. History in `deals`: `briefUpdates`, `briefFullAt`. `new_since` is returned but not shown (Blake, 2026-11-07: not on the card or the note; kept for "anything new?" answers later). Before: any change reread everything (51455 on 2026-11-07: 25 items, 9,337 tokens in, 3,714 out, 37 seconds).

- What the record leaves out before a model reads it (2026-11-11, from a read of the 14 open Utah deals; rules in `callText` and `textMessage`, `src/hubspot/dealContext.js`):
  - Calls: Aloware's header lines (who called whom, Line, From or Destination, Creator Type) and the "Call with <name>" title every call carries. When HubSpot and Aloware both summarized a call, only Aloware's "Call Summary" is read; HubSpot's is used when Aloware wrote none. A call nobody summarized (93 of 197, most of them missed or unanswered) becomes one short line such as "Missed call from the Roamer; not answered."
  - Texts: the "Click here to respond" line after a message, log entries with no message (a picture message leaves one), a message that is just "null", and Aloware's two automated replies (after hours, and missed call). A bare link is kept. A Roamer's reply to an automated text is kept.
  - Emails and notes are unchanged; no automated email turned up on those deals.
  - Changing these rules changes what every saved brief was built from, so each deal's next brief is a full read.
- A brief update returns only the parts it changes (2026-11-11): the model names them in `changed` and writes those in full; `applyChanges` in `src/agents/roamerBrief.js` merges them into the saved brief. An update that names a part and hands it back empty, or does not leave a valid brief, is refused and the whole record is reread. Not yet run against the live model.
- An edited older item is found by a hash of what each item says, kept in the brief's signature (2026-11-11), so HubSpot moving an item's modified date without a word changing no longer forces a full read. A brief saved before the hash existed still goes by the date.

## Questions (/ask)
- **Q&A reuse in shadow mode: live and verified 2026-11-07** on 51455 (a repeat of the awning arm question reused "the 10th of November", marked Right; "tracking on the awning arm?" filed as a follow-up in the same note; "trailer color" new; about $0.0026 per question on a deal with earlier Q&A, routing plus the reuse check). On `/ask` in a deal space with earlier Q&A notes, Haiku (`src/agents/qaReuse.js`, beside the routing call) reads the deal's Q&A notes from HubSpot and decides: the same question already answered (it names the note and quotes the answer, who, and when, and whether it is a fact or a status), a follow-up on an earlier topic, or new. Matching is by meaning, so "awning arm arrival" and "awning arm tracking" land together. Code applies the Deal Knowledge rule: a fact is reused at any age, a status only within 48 hours of when it was answered (Basecamp's `answeredAt`, else the note's date). The asker sees nothing new in shadow mode (still routed). Blake's routed-question card shows the suggested answer with Send this answer (Right) and Not right (Wrong), or why it would not reuse (too old), or that it is a follow-up. The question is filed in the earlier topic's note instead of a new one. The database keeps `reuse` (decision, note, kind, still good, cost) and `reuseVerdict`, never the text; the Right/Wrong tally is the evidence for item 6.
- **Q&A answers in the space**, once 5 is proven. The bar (Blake, 2026-11-08): 10 Right/Wrong verdicts on reusable answers from real Team Member questions, at most 1 Wrong. 8 verdicts so far (2026-11-07), all Right, all on Blake's own test questions, so none count toward the bar yet: 4 from earlier answers, 4 from the brief (walkthrough attendance asked twice; the 8th, the tow vehicle, sent from the new card). The traps passed (Blake). One, on a 51422 delivery date question, was tapped Wrong by mistake and corrected to Right in Firestore by hand, with `reuseVerdictCorrectedFrom` kept on the record. Basecamp may not answer on its own yet: the bar is not met (real Team Member questions are needed), and CLAUDE.md also wants answers proven against LightSpeed, which is not reachable (item 20). The brief is a second source (built 2026-11-07, Blake): Haiku (`src/agents/briefAnswer.js`) checks the question against the deal's saved brief beside the earlier-answer check; a Team Member's earlier answer still good wins, then the brief's; Blake sees it as the suggested answer on the routed-question card; a brief status counts only within 48 hours of its source item and never without a date; the reuse record keeps `source: "brief"`. Brief verdicts count toward the bar (Blake, 2026-11-07), tallied separately by `source`.

## /wow
- **LightSpeed is a third source for a suggested answer (built 2026-11-11, not yet tried on a real question).** When a question is on a deal and lookups are on, Basecamp reads the deal and its repair orders from LightSpeed (`facts.read` in `src/factsJob.js`, at most 8 seconds, beside routing) and Haiku (`src/agents/factsAnswer.js`) decides whether those facts state the answer. Order: a Team Member's earlier answer still good wins (a person can know a job is done; LightSpeed cannot), then LightSpeed, then the brief. A LightSpeed answer is always fresh, since it was read a moment ago. Blake sees it as the suggested answer, "From LightSpeed's <part>, read just now", with Send this answer (Right) and Not right (Wrong); the reuse record keeps `source: "lightspeed"`, so these verdicts are tallied on their own. The model reads a trimmed deal: no labor prices, no repair order "in" date (LightSpeed's can fall after the work closed), tech notes only where there is no resolution, and a count of clock sessions over 10 hours (a clock left on). Its instructions forbid saying a job, mod, or PDI is done, since nothing in LightSpeed says so. LightSpeed being down, slow, or without the deal never stops a question. It is one fixed read and one model call, not an agent choosing its steps: the deal is already known, so there is nothing to choose.
- **`/wow`: live 2026-11-07, first test failed on quality and was reworked; the library moved to the ROA WOW Ideas sheet (`basecamp-00053-6q5`); retest passed 2026-11-07 (Blake likes the sheet link for additions).** In a deal space (or "/wow 51455" in a DM), for people on `AI_USERS`. Answers at once, then posts a card in the thread: 2 or 3 WOW ideas from Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`src/agents/wowPlan.js`), tailored from the deal's saved brief and ROA's WOW idea library, each with what to do, the brief detail it rests on, cost tier, and owner, and "Use this one". A tap saves the plan as a note on the Delivery record and the Roamer (first line "Basecamp WOW plan: ...", so Marketing can count them; Blake chose notes over WOW fields, 2026-11-07) and remembers the idea in the library in its general form (a library idea's use count goes up; a new one is added) so it can be re-tailored for other deliveries. "/wow ideas" lists the library, most used first (no AI, anyone allowed). Quick command 6, "WOW ideas" in the + menu, is /wow with nothing typed (a quick command carries no text); /wow stays a slash command for "/wow ideas" and "/wow 51455". Later quick commands, each registered only when its code is ready: "Roamer brief" (a deal space's own brief, with briefs in spaces) and "Anything new?" (item 9). The library is the team's Google Sheet "ROA WOW Ideas" (Blake's call, 2026-11-07; `WOW_SHEET_ID` 1aQ3f46vWKeCcRyesMiRfZQUle81jIHM1mpJwmGsVpUI, in Blake's Drive, shared with `basecamp-router` as an editor; Sheets API enabled). The Ideas tab has Idea (general, no names), Fits when (the kind of Roamer detail that makes it right, which is what the model matches the brief against), Kind, Tier, Active, Times used, Added by; the "Standard at every delivery" tab lists the baseline Basecamp never suggests. Basecamp reads both on every `/wow` (about 30 tokens an idea, so a few hundred ideas still fit in one read), adds 1 to Times used when a library idea is chosen, and appends a row (Added by "Basecamp", with its own Fits when) when a new idea is chosen (`src/wowLibrary.js`). Without the sheet it falls back to the database library (`wowIdeas`) and the list in `wowPlan.js`. A real menu with costs is for Blake and Shane to build in the sheet. Fixed the same day: `STANDARD_AT_DELIVERY` is never suggested (Blake's list: the TV welcome screen with the Roamer's name and trailer images, a long walkthrough, lunch, a full LP tank, fresh water if asked, a gift bag with camping chairs, mugs, and plates, and a one-year onX trip-planning subscription); tailoring must rest on a need, worry, goal, plan, gear, or a stated favorite in the brief (Blake's example: a towing and backing practice course for a first-time owner), not a name dropped into a generic idea; the first starter set was switched off in the database (kept, `active` false) and replaced with a second set built around confidence, skills, and the first trip. ## Changing the brief (Blake, 2026-11-12)
- Two kinds of change make every saved brief stop matching, so each deal's next brief is a full paid read (about $0.18 a deal, about $2.50 across 14 open Utah deals): a change to the brief's shape (a part added, removed, renamed, or retyped), and a change to what the record leaves out or how an item reads (`src/hubspot/dealContext.js`). A change to the instructions' wording alone forces nothing; a saved brief picks it up on its next update or full read.
- Both kinds are frozen during live testing. Wanted changes are collected and shipped together as one release, so the team pays one sweep, not one per idea.
- Try a change on a branch with `/brief` in Claude Code first: it builds the record and reads the instructions and shape from the working tree, on the subscription, with no API credits, and saves nothing to HubSpot or Basecamp's records. It does not prove the API result (a different model setting writes it, and brief updates are not covered), so one paid side by side on one deal comes before the release.
- `test/briefGuard.test.js` fails when either kind of change is made, with the cost in its message. Its pins are updated only in the release that ships the batch.
- Adding a part does not force the sweep (built 2026-11-12, `OPTIONAL_PARTS` in `src/agents/roamerBrief.js`; no part is registered yet). A saved brief without the new part stays valid; the card leaves the part off; the deal's next update is told the part is missing and writes it, at an update's cost. It is written from the saved brief and the new items, not the whole record, so it is thinner until the deal's next full read (after 10 updates or 30 days). To add one: the part and its instructions in `SCHEMA` and `SYSTEM`, its name and type check in `OPTIONAL_PARTS`, its line in the HubSpot note (`src/agents/briefNote.js`, left unset when the line is absent), and a card that draws without it. Removing, renaming, or retyping a part still forces the sweep.
