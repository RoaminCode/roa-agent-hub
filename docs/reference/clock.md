# The touchpoint clock

Business days before projected delivery. Decided with Blake 2026-11-07; in design, not built. Read before building any touchpoint.

- Build now, in shadow mode (Blake, 2026-11-07): the delivery date Basecamp reads in HubSpot is LightSpeed's, so the clock needs no proving against LightSpeed first.
- When the deal space is opened on demand from the Chat app (which also finds or creates the Delivery record): welcome and profile draft. Nothing happens at deposit. Then: weekly update once the trailer has a confirmed in-the-shop date after PDI; 7 Delivery Coach assigned; 5 walkthrough video; 4 tell the Delivery Coach delivery is 4 days out and suggest a brief update (briefs stay on request only, never automatic); 3 Delivery Coach intro; 1 date and time confirmed plus showroom check; delivery day walkthrough with no extra message.
- Touchpoints 2 to 8 accepted as listed (Blake, 2026-11-07).
- PDI and the in-shop date live in notes on the Delivery record. A PDI note starts with "PDI:" and the date ("PDI: 11/12"); Team Members and Basecamp both write it that way, so code finds it with no AI. If no PDI note exists 2 business days after the brief is created, Basecamp asks the Service Advisor whether PDI is scheduled and notes the answer with the scheduled date on the Delivery record (Blake, 2026-11-07). Deals with no space have no brief and get no PDI check. The weekly update starts once a note confirms the in-shop date.
- The RJB Agent Spec's "Touchpoint clock" section matches this list (updated 2026-11-07).
- The showroom check reads the Delivery record's "In the Showroom!" stage. The Service Advisor moves it, by hand in HubSpot or through the Chat app (Blake, 2026-11-07; the Chat app move is not built, and is a new HubSpot write the CLAUDE.md rules must name before it is built).
